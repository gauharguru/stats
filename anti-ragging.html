What counts as ragging?

🗣️

Verbal

Teasing, abusing, insulting or threatening a student, in person or online.

✋

Physical

Any act that causes physical harm, fear or embarrassment.

📱

Psychological

Forcing a student to do anything against their will, isolation, or exploitation.

Our policy

How we keep students safe

  • The college follows the UGC Regulations on Curbing the Menace of Ragging in Higher Educational Institutions, 2009
  • Every student and parent submits an anti-ragging undertaking at admission
  • An Anti-Ragging Committee and Squad monitor the campus and hostels
  • Punishment can include suspension, expulsion, withholding of results and a police complaint
