How to get a hostel seat.

Hostel admission uses the official AHS Hostel Admission Form (8 pages).

1

Download the form

Print the Hostel Admission Form (PDF).

2

Fill it in

Student, guardian and medical details, with an emergency treatment consent signed by the guardian.

3

Get the affidavit notarised

The hostel affidavit (pages 6–8: anti-ragging, anti-drug and discipline) is signed before a Notary Public.

4

Submit at the office

Hand in the form with the documents below and pay the fee. The office allots your room.

Documents to attach

  • Aadhaar card (student)
  • Aadhaar card (guardian)
  • Admission letter
  • 4 passport-size photos
  • Medical fitness certificate
  • Anti-ragging affidavit
  • Hostel affidavit (draft attached to the form)
  • Previous academic records
  • Fee receipt
  • Character certificate (if asked)

Hostel rules.

Accepted by every student and guardian on the admission form. Tap a heading to read the rules.

Discipline & conduct
  • Respectful behaviour; no violence, bullying or harassment
  • Ragging in any form is strictly prohibited and legally punishable
  • Decent dress code inside hostel premises
Timings & attendance
  • Daily roll-call / biometric attendance is compulsory
  • No entry or exit beyond permitted hours without written permission
  • Night-out only with guardian approval
Room & property
  • Do not damage furniture, fittings, walls or electrical items
  • Students pay full replacement cost for damage they cause
  • Keep rooms, bathrooms and corridors clean
Prohibited items
  • Alcohol, drugs, tobacco and cigarettes
  • Weapons, explosives and firecrackers
  • Induction stoves, heaters, rods and high-voltage appliances
  • Pornography, gambling items or any illegal material
Visitors
  • Only parents / guardians may visit
  • No visitors inside rooms; meetings in designated areas only
Mess & food
  • Follow mess timings
  • Food wastage is punishable
  • Outside food as per the warden's instructions
Safety & emergency
  • Do not tamper with fire alarms, extinguishers or CCTV
  • Report suspicious activity immediately
  • Fire and emergency drills are mandatory
Grounds for expulsion
  • Ragging, substance abuse, violence, theft or property damage
  • Repeated indiscipline or tampering with security systems
  • Unauthorised stay outside the hostel, or anti-national activity

Hostel fees are non-refundable except the security deposit. Late payment may attract a fine or suspension of hostel privileges.
