Our storyCulture

A college built around a hospital.

AHS Nursing College & Hospital is a unit of Sir Syed Educational & Charitable Trust. We started with a simple idea: nurses learn best at the bedside. So our students train at our own AHS Global Hospital from their first year, and our teachers work alongside doctors every day.

Since our approvals in 2023–24 we have grown to 180 INC-approved seats, run mass casualty drills, fire safety training with the Bihar Fire Service, bedside case presentations and community health camps — and in 2026 we were honoured by the Government of Bihar.
