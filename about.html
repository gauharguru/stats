Who we are

A nursing college with its own hospital.

AHS Nursing College & Hospital is run by Sir Syed Educational Trust (registered under the Society Registration Act 1920). The college is affiliated to the Indian Nursing Council, Bihar University of Health Sciences and the Bihar Nurses Registration Council, and is recognised by the Government of Bihar.

We offer ANM, GNM and B.Sc Nursing programmes. With an attached hospital on our doorstep, students move from classroom to lab to ward with the support of experienced faculty.
