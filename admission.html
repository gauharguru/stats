Admission

How to get into AHS.

Admission is through the AHS Entrance Exam (NEST), the BUHS Joint Entrance Test or BCECEB, as applicable, with due weight to your academic record.

How to ApplyBSCC & ScholarshipsFee Structure
Step 1

Get the form

Download it free from this website, or buy it for ₹1,000 cash at the Counselling Cell.

Step 2

Pay & submit

Online: ₹1,000 for application, or ₹11,000 for application and registration.

Step 3

Entrance & merit

Written test, interview and group discussion for Bachelor courses, as applicable.

Step 4

Confirm your seat

Pay the balance fee within the given time. ₹10,000 is adjusted in the course fee.

Admission procedure

Submitting by post

Fill in every entry with the right course codes, and send the form with a Demand Draft of ₹1,000 (application) or ₹11,000 (application and registration) in favour of “AHS Nursing College”, payable at Samastipur, along with photocopies of the documents listed in the form, by Registered Post / Speed Post to:

AHS Nursing College
Jail Chowk, Tajpur Road, Samastipur, Bihar – 848129, India

Please note

  • The last date to receive completed application forms is 30 October. Late applications will not be accepted without a valid reason.
  • Incomplete forms will not be considered. Application fees are not refundable.
  • The college is not responsible for postal delay or loss in transit.
  • Final allotment is by the cumulative merit of the AHS Entrance Score and 10+2 score.
  • If the college cannot offer admission, the registration fee is refunded after a 10% processing charge.
