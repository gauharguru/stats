Admission Process

  1. Collect / download the application form.
  2. Submit with mark sheets, ID proof, photographs and medical certificate.
  3. Entrance / merit-based selection as per council norms.
  4. Document verification and fee payment.

BSCC – Bihar Student Credit Card

Eligible students can finance their course fee under the Bihar Student Credit Card scheme (up to ₹4 lakh education loan).

  • Apply online at the 7 Nishchay portal.
  • Visit the District Registration & Counselling Centre (DRCC) for verification.
  • The college office provides admission letter, fee structure and bonafide certificate for BSCC.
