One INC-approved qualification. A career anywhere.

INCApproved by the Indian Nursing Council, New Delhi · 60 seats in each course 🏆 Awarded by the Government of Bihar for taking INC affiliation

The Indian Nursing Council sets the standard for nursing education in India. Its approval is what makes your ANM, GNM or B.Sc Nursing count — across India, in government service, and as the base qualification for a nursing licence abroad.
