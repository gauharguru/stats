Admission · BSCC

Study now. Pay later.

Eligible students from Bihar can fund their nursing course through the Bihar Student Credit Card (BSCC) scheme.

BSCCFee Structure
Step 1

Register online

Apply on the official 7 Nishchay portal.

Step 2

Visit DRCC

Verify documents at your District Registration & Counselling Centre.

Step 3

Loan approval

The loan is sanctioned by the Bihar State Education Finance Corporation.

Step 4

Fees paid

Fees are paid directly to the college.

Documents usually needed

  • 10th and 12th marksheets and certificates
  • Aadhaar card and residence certificate (Bihar)
  • Admission letter and fee structure from the college
  • Bank passbook, photographs and co-applicant documents

Loan limits, interest and documents are set by the Government of Bihar and may change. Always check the official portal. Our office helps you with the college documents.
