AHS Global Hospital

A 100+ bed hospital in Samastipur run by the same trust. Students train here on real wards from their first year.

  • 24-hour emergency and OPD — Medical, Surgical, Obstetrics & Gynaecology, Ophthalmology, Paediatrics, Psychology
  • Maternity care, labour room, newborn care and vaccination
  • Day-care admissions with pathology support
  • Door-to-door primary care, health awareness programmes and check-up camps
