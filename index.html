01The INC advantage

One INC-approved qualification. A career anywhere.

INCApproved by the Indian Nursing Council, New Delhi · 60 seats in each course

The Indian Nursing Council sets the standard for nursing education in India. Its approval is what makes your ANM, GNM or B.Sc Nursing count — across India, in government service, and as the base qualification for a nursing licence abroad.

🇮🇳

Recognised across India

Register with your State Nursing Council and work as a nurse in any state of India.

🏛️

State & central government jobs

Apply for staff nurse and nursing officer posts in Bihar and other states, and in central government hospitals like AIIMS.

✈️

Your gateway abroad

Overseas nursing regulators check for INC-recognised training before you sit their licensing exam.

🎓

Higher studies & teaching

Go on to Post Basic B.Sc, M.Sc Nursing or a PhD, and teach in nursing institutions.
