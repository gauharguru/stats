Labs & libraries

Practise every skill before you meet a patient.

Our labs are set up the way a real ward is, so you build confidence step by step.

  • Nursing foundation, community health and MCH labs
  • Anatomy, nutrition and computer labs
  • A library with nursing textbooks and journals

Clinical training

Learn at the bedside, from day one.

Our attached hospital gives you supervised practice in real wards, OPD and community settings.

  • Supervised ward postings each semester
  • Community health visits and health camps
  • Training in maternity, paediatric and medical-surgical care
