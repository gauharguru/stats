01The INC advantage

One INC-approved qualification. A career anywhere.

INCApproved by the Indian Nursing Council, New Delhi · 60 seats in each course 🏆 Awarded by the Government of Bihar for taking INC affiliation

The Indian Nursing Council sets the standard for nursing education in India. Its approval is what makes your ANM, GNM or B.Sc Nursing count — across India, in government service, and as the base qualification for a nursing licence abroad.

🇮🇳

Recognised across India

Register with your State Nursing Council and work as a nurse in any state of India.

🏛️

State & central government jobs

Apply for staff nurse and nursing officer posts in Bihar and other states, and in central government hospitals like AIIMS.

✈️

Your gateway abroad

Overseas nursing regulators check for INC-recognised training before you sit their licensing exam.

🎓

Higher studies & teaching

Go on to Post Basic B.Sc, M.Sc Nursing or a PhD, and teach in nursing institutions.

From 12th to a career in care, step by step.

01/ 06
📘
Step 1

You finish 12th

Science with Biology opens B.Sc Nursing. Any stream with English opens GNM. Girls from any stream can choose ANM.

Step 2

You join AHS

Admission through NEST, the BUHS Joint Entrance Test or BCECEB. The Bihar Student Credit Card covers GNM and B.Sc fees.

Step 3

You practise in our labs

Ten specialised labs — anatomy, MCH, simulation and more — so every skill is rehearsed before you meet a patient.

Step 4

You train in a live hospital

Real wards, OPD, emergency and labour room at AHS Global Hospital, our own 100-bed hospital, plus government hospitals across Bihar.

🪪
Step 5

You get INC-recognised

Your INC-approved qualification lets you register with the State Nursing Council and work anywhere in India.

Step 6

You build your career

AIIMS and government hospitals, private hospitals — or Europe, the Gulf, the USA and Australia after their licensing exams.
